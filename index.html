Cards and Coffee Support

Cards and Coffee includes Cribbage, Euchre, Hearts, Spades, and Phase Rummy for iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. Play offline against computer opponents, save unfinished games, or use two-device nearby Cribbage.

Report a problem

Email: CardsAndCoffee.app@gmail.com

Include the device model, OS version, app version/build, game, orientation, and steps that caused the issue. Screenshots can help; avoid including private information or another player's concealed cards.

Nearby Cribbage

Enable Wi-Fi on both devices, keep the app open, and allow Local Network access. On both devices open Cribbage > Play Nearby. Host on one device and join on the other. Compare all eight digits of the connection code before accepting. If discovery fails, stop and restart nearby play. No internet connection is required; some managed Wi-Fi networks may interfere with nearby traffic.

Saved games and statistics

Each game has its own saved-game options. Open the game's menu to resume or delete its saved game. Statistics can be reset separately by game.
